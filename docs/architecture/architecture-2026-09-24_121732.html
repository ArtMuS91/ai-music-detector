Phase 3 — ML detection service · in progress Generated 2026-09-24 12:17:32 web/ src/ · .NET 10 PostgreSQL 17

A pasted YouTube link becomes a queued job that a single in-process worker downloads with yt-dlp, normalizes with ffmpeg and researches via Groq. The verdict stays Inconclusive until aggregation exists.

ClaimFOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED picks the oldest Pending row and sets Acquiring in one transaction.
CleanupAudio is deleted on Completed/Failed and on requeue: preprocessed WAV first, then the download folder.
Cancellation & recoveryOn shutdown the job stays in progress; at worker start RecoverInterruptedAsync requeues it to Pending and clears Track.
Failure reasonA UserFacingException message, else a generic per-stage text, capped at 2048 chars. If saving fails, the job is recorded Failed with no result.
